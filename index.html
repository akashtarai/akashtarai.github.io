Biography

Dr. Akash Kumar Tarai is currently a Research Fellow at the Centre for Optical and Laser Engineering (COLE), School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore.

Previously, he served as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Department of Physical Sciences, IISER Berhampur, India (Dec 2024 – Sep 2025), and as a Visiting Scholar at the Research School of Earth Sciences, Australian National University (ANU) (May – Oct 2024), supported by the Future Research Talent (FRT) Award.

He earned his Ph.D. in Physics from the University of Hyderabad (2024). His thesis focused on the development of a LIBS-based elemental analysis technique and the integration of machine learning for real-time spectroscopic applications. He also holds an M.Sc. in Physics from Pondicherry University, India and a B.Sc. in Physics from Ravenshaw University, India.

Dr. Tarai has co-authored 19 publications (14 peer-reviewed articles, 3 book chapters & 2 conference proceedings) and presented his work at several international and national conferences. His contributions to the applications of laser spectroscopy have been recognized through awards such as Best Thesis Award, Best Poster Award. He also recieved the prestigious Future Research Talent (FRT) Award from Australian National University, Australia in 2024 and selected for the National Post Doctoral Fellowship (N-PDF) 2025 by the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF), Govt. of India.
