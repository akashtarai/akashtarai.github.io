Biography

I am currently a Research Fellow at the Centre for Optical and Laser Engineering (COLE), School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. My research focuses on fundamental studies of laser-matter interaction, ultrafast laser processing, laser spectroscopy, optical diagnostics, and the integration of machine learning for advanced spectroscopic and sensing applications.

Previously, I worked as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Department of Physical Sciences, Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Berhampur (Dec 2024 – Sep 2025). I was also a Visiting Scholar at the Research School of Earth Sciences, Australian National University (May – Oct 2024), where my research was supported by the prestigious Future Research Talent (FRT) Award.

I received my Ph.D. in Physics from University of Hyderabad in 2024. My doctoral research focused on the development of LIBS-based elemental analysis techniques and fundamental studies of laser-produced plasma along with and the development and instrumentation of laser spectroscopic systems (particularly LIBS and Raman spectroscopy) with integration of machine learning for real-time sensing applications. I also hold an M.Sc. in Physics from Pondicherry University, India and a B.Sc. in Physics from Ravenshaw University, India.

My contributions to laser spectroscopy and optical diagnostics have been recognized through honors including the Best Thesis Award, Best Poster Award, and the Future Research Talent (FRT) Award 2024 from Australian National University. I was also selected for the prestigious National Post Doctoral Fellowship (N-PDF) 2025 by the Anusandhan National Research Foundation, Government of India.
